Start the Guide

Your 10-Step Guide

Follow one step at a time. Keep things simple and focus on consistency.

1

Set Your Goal

Choose what you want to achieve: lose weight, maintain your weight, or reduce body fat.

2

Eat the Right Amount

If weight loss is your goal, aim for a small calorie deficit. Avoid extreme diets.

3

Eat More Protein

Include foods such as eggs, chicken, fish, lean meat, Greek yoghurt, beans or tofu.

4

Choose Healthy Foods

Build meals around protein, fruit and vegetables, carbohydrates and healthy fats.

5

Drink Water

Drink water regularly and try to limit sugary drinks.

6

Stay Active

Walk more and choose activities you enjoy. Exercise does not have to be complicated.

7

Build Strength

Do strength exercises regularly to help build and maintain muscle.

8

Track Progress

Check your weight, measurements and fitness progress. Look at changes over time.

9

Sleep Well

Aim for around 7–9 hours of sleep each night.

10

Stay Consistent

You do not need to be perfect. Get back to your routine after a bad day.

What is NutriStep?

NutriStep is a simple weight-management guide designed to help people build healthier habits without complicated diets or extreme rules. Our goal is to make weight management simple, realistic and achievable — one small step at a time.
